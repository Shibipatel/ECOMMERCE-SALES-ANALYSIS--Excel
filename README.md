# 📊 Ecommerce Sales Analysis Dashboard (Excel)

## 📁 Project Overview
This project presents an *Ecommerce Sales Analysis Dashboard, designed using **Microsoft Excel*. 
The dashboard provides interactive visual insights into sales and profit trends across months, category-wise profit contribution, regional performance across US states, top sub-categories, and overall business KPIs.

---

## 📈 Dashboard Highlights
The dashboard includes the following key visualizations:

1. *KPI Cards (YoY Performance)*  
   Displays total Sales ($2.29M, +20.62% YoY), Profit ($286.40K, +14.41% YoY), Quantity sold (37.87K, +27.45% YoY), Total Orders (9,994, +28.64% YoY), and Profit     Margin (12%).

2. *Sales and Profit Analysis (Monthly)*  
   Combo chart displaying total monthly sales alongside profit to identify peak sales periods and seasonality.

3. *Category Wise Profit*  
   Waterfall breakdown showing profit contributions across Technology ($145.45K), Office Supplies ($122.49K), and Furniture ($18.45K).

4. *Category Wise Sales Share %*  
   Donut chart highlighting the distribution of total revenue across major product categories.

5. *Sales by State*  
   Geographic map visualization highlighting revenue distribution across US states.

6. *Top 5 Subcategories by Sales*  
   Horizontal bar chart ranking the highest revenue-generating subcategories (Phones, Chairs, Storage, Tables, Binders).

---

## 💡 Key Insights
The dashboard reveals the following key takeaways:

1. *Technology* is the primary profit driver, contributing *$145.45K* out of the total $286.40K profit.
2. *Furniture* yields the lowest profit margin, generating only *$18.45K* despite holding a substantial share of total sales (~32%).
3. *Phones ($330.01K)* and *Chairs ($328.45K)* are the top two revenue-generating sub-categories.
4. *Order volume and quantity* saw significant annual momentum, both growing over *27% YoY*.
5. *Fall/Winter (Sept–Dec)* represents the peak sales period of the year.

> *Filter Data:* Use the interactive slicers on the right (Year, Region, Segment) to filter and update all chart metrics dynamically.

## 🎯 Final Conclusion & Business Recommendations
* *Optimize Furniture Margins:* Review pricing, discount thresholds, and shipping overhead for the Furniture category (especially Tables), as its profit conversion is disproportionately low relative to its sales volume.
* *Capitalize on High-Margin Categories:* Increase promotional focus and bundle deals on *Technology* and top-performing subcategories like *Phones* to maximize bottom-line profit.
